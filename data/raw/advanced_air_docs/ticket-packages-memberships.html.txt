How packages work
Sold in 
bundles of 10 only
, valid for ten one-way tickets on the selected route at a preset discounted rate. All applicable taxes are included.
Tickets are 
valid only for the city pair purchased
, and can be used in either direction and for multiple passengers on that route.
Tickets are 
fully transferable to any guest
 of your choosing.
Individual tickets are 
changeable and fully refundable back to your account
 if changed or cancelled at least 30 minutes before scheduled departure. Funds for later cancellations are forfeited.
Includes activation of a 
personal portal
 to manage individual reservations.
Seating is provided on a 
space-available basis
 and is not guaranteed.
Packages that include Hawthorne (HHR) flights come with a 
50% discount on all parking fees
 at Hawthorne Airport.
Package purchases are non-refundable
, travel must be completed by the expiration date, and any residual value at expiration is forfeited.
Completing a ticket package purchase signifies your agreement to the

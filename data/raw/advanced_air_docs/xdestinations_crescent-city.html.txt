The shortcut
The Redwoods stop being a someday trip.
Crescent City is the only town with scheduled air service that lands in Redwood country — CEC sits about four miles from downtown, and Howland Hill Road, the dirt lane through Jedediah Smith old growth, starts just east of town. Fly to Arcata or Eureka instead and you're still about 80 miles and an hour and a half south of the old growth.
This page works in both directions. If you're one of the roughly 6,700 people who live here — or the 27,000 across Del Norte County — the same aircraft is how Crescent City reaches Bay Area hospitals, universities and agencies, or LA family and LAX connections, without giving US-101 a whole day each way. Parking at CEC is free. Every day. Ask any airport in America how rare that is.
And a local's note before you check the forecast: the morning fog isn't bad weather. It's how a redwood drinks.
Why Crescent City
The trees, the tide, the harbor.
Muir Woods is the appetizer. This is the meal.
Summer
Old growth at trunk level
Howland Hill Road runs 10 unpaved miles through Jedediah Smith old growth — trees over a thousand years old, minutes from the runway. Stout Grove is a half-mile loop; the Grove of Titans boardwalk runs 1,300 feet through some of the largest coast redwoods ever measured.
Year-round
A lighthouse the ocean schedules
Battery Point Lighthouse (1856) sits on a tidal island — you cross the land bridge only at low tide. Tours run 10–4 daily April–September and weekends October–March, tide permitting. Check the table, then walk across the ocean floor.
Spring · Fall
A hundred thousand birds a day
Tolowa Dunes State Park logs 250-plus species on the Pacific Flyway — 27 miles of trails, 11 miles of open beach. Gray whales pass the Klamath River Overlook December–January and March–April, with a resident feeding pod on clear summer days.
Nov–Jul
Crab town, honestly
This is a working Dungeness harbor — sea lions run the south docks year-round, and the recreational crab season in the northern zone typically runs November to July. Openings shift with health and whale advisories, so check CDFW before you plan around it.
One runway, two coastlines
Pick your base.

From the runway to the trees
CEC sits about four miles from downtown, minutes from Jedediah Smith trailheads. Avis and Budget rent at the airport — pre-book before you fly, because there's no Uber or Lyft ecosystem here. Redwood Coast Transit's Dial-A-Ride covers the airport and town for $6 — (707) 308-RIDE.
Check-in, coast edition
Parking at CEC is free, every day — just notify the airport if you're leaving a car beyond about two weeks. Arrive two hours before departure with bags checked at least 45 minutes out. On the LA end, Hawthorne runs a free LAX shuttle with 48-plus hours' notice; HHR parking is limited at about $50 a day.

Quick answers
Crescent City, answered.
Which airport does Advanced Air fly to in Crescent City?
Del Norte County Regional Airport — Jack McNamara Field (CEC), about four miles from downtown and minutes from Redwood National & State Parks trailheads. Parking is free, every day. Flying to Arcata or Eureka instead leaves you about 80 miles and an hour and a half south of the Jedediah Smith old growth.
Can I use Crescent City as the airport for the southern Oregon coast?
Yes — CEC is the closest scheduled-service airport to the far-southern Oregon coast. Brookings sits about 30 minutes north with no airline of its own; the Boardman corridor viewpoints and Gold Beach run 45 minutes to an hour up US-101. Medford is about 2.5 hours inland, North Bend about 2 hours 20 north.
How long is the flight to Crescent City?
About 1 hour 6 minutes to or from Oakland. On the Los Angeles route, about 1 hour 42 from Crescent City to Hawthorne and about 2 hours northbound, per the current schedule. The drives those flights replace run 6–7 hours from the Bay Area and 12–14 from LA.
What days do flights operate?
Days vary by season. The current summer pattern: Oakland flies near-daily, with a Friday double; Hawthorne runs a Thursday/Sunday core plus Friday and Saturday summer additions. The timetables linked on this page render from our live scheduling system, so trust them over anything printed.
Do I need a car?
Yes, for the parks — reserve Avis or Budget at CEC before you fly. Redwood Coast Transit's $6 Dial-A-Ride covers the airport and town — (707) 308-RIDE — but there's no Uber or Lyft ecosystem here, and the trailheads need wheels. Pre-booking the car is the whole trick.
What's the weather like? Will it be foggy?
Cool and honest. Summer highs run low-to-mid 60s with morning marine fog that usually burns off inland — the redwoods drink it, literally. June through September is driest; late August and September are the warm, clear sweet spot; November through March is storm season.
What should I pack?
Layers, year-round: a warm mid-layer, a rain shell, and shoes you can get wet — Fern Canyon runs ankle-to-knee deep in 2026. Summer on this coast is not summer California; 60 degrees and wind is a normal July afternoon. The fog is part of the show.
How do I get into the redwoods from the airport?
Drive Howland Hill Road — the 10-mile unpaved lane through Jedediah Smith old growth starts just east of town (no RVs or trailers). Stout Grove and the Grove of Titans boardwalk are both off it. Most of Redwood National & State Parks charges no entrance fee; Fern Canyon needs a free advance vehicle permit May 15–September 15 plus a day-use fee, and state-park day-use areas post their own fees.
When can I walk to Battery Point Lighthouse?
Only at low tide — the 1856 lighthouse sits on a tidal island. Tours run 10 AM–4 PM daily April through September and weekends October through March, tide permitting. Check a Crescent City tide table the morning you go; the ocean keeps the schedule, not the lighthouse.
What about crabbing and fishing?
Crescent City is a working Dungeness harbor. The recreational crab season in the northern zone typically runs November 1 to July 30, but openings shift with health and whale advisories — always check CDFW before planning around it. Smith River steelhead runs under low-flow rules September 1–April 30, barbless hooks only.
I live here — how does this work for Bay Area or LA trips?
Park free at CEC, fly to Oakland in about an hour for appointments, universities or family, or to Hawthorne for LA — with a free shuttle to LAX (book 48-plus hours ahead) for connections anywhere. Arrive two hours early; bags are checked at least 45 minutes before departure.

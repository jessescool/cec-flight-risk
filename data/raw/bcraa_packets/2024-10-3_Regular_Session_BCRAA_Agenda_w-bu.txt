FROM:           Ryan Cooley, Airport Director
SUBJECT:        Wind Direction Indicator Payment

RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
Approve a fixed asset payment in the amount of $9,438.09 for the purchase and installation of a new secondary wind direction
Indicator, as budgeted for in the FY 2024-2025 BCRAA approved budget, at the request of the Del Norte County Auditor-
Controller’s office. **

DISCUSSION/SUMMARY:
In March of 2024 the secondary wind direction indicator at Jack McNamara Field was damaged beyond repair by a large
winter storm. This is a mandatory airfield nav-aid required by the FAA. The procurement and installation were budgeted
for and included in the approved FY 24-25 budget. The Del Norte County Auditor-Controller’s office is requiring board
approval of the payment as it is considered a Fixed Asset line item.

ALTERNATIVES:
Do not approve the payment.

Memorandum re Agenda Item #5.

September 30, 2024

To: BCRAA Commissioners
From: Robert Black. General Counsel
Re: Increased appropriations for legal and consulting services to pursue CEC
      Terminal issues.

Chair Finigan and Commissioners in session:

As you know, BCRAA has retained the firms of JarvisFay, Attorneys and Jay
Carey Consulting, design and construction consultants. Substantial effort has been
expended and I believe progress has been made in addressing certain fitness issues
at the Terminal. We (all parties) now have general agreement on the name of a
mediator and are in the process of conflict checking toward retaining her. A
separate agreement and budget will be needed when we have the mediator in place.

Since the current “not-to-exceed” limits within our legal and consulting
agreements have been or are near exhaustion, I am recommending the following
increases to those limits so that our efforts are not stalled interrupted: An increase
of $35,000 to the JarvisFay contract and an increase of $15,000 to the Carey
Consulting contract.

Recommendation: That you direct your General Counsel to prepare and your Chair

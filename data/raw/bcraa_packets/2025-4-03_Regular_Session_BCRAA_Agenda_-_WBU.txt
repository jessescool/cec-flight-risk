9. Receive Airport Director’s Report

          The Airport Director provided updates on various airport matters, including an update on the
           outstanding invoices from Advanced Air, the delivery and status of the ARFF truck, the timeline and
           steps for the five-year consultant selection process, funding efforts and next steps for the fuel farm
           replacement project, and the successful Advanced Air one-year anniversary event, including the
           announcement that Advanced Air now has a codeshare agreement in place with Alaska Airlines.

10. Receive update on carrier enplanements and on time performance statistics.

          The Board reviewed the February 2025 Airline Activity Report for Crescent City (CEC). The report
           showed that all 72 scheduled flight segments were completed, with 36 total aircraft arrivals. A total
           of 475 passengers were enplaned and 480 deplaned, including both revenue and non-revenue
           travelers. The average load factor across all routes was 44.21%. The airport recorded five
           controllable delays and three uncontrollable delays, with an overall completion rate of 100% and a
           controllable on-time performance of 93.06%. Year-to-date enplanements reached 1,013, reflecting
           a 12.29% increase over the previous year.

11. Receive reports from Commissioners regarding Border Coast Regional Airport Authority business, request
    clarification, information or that items be included in the future agendas.

              Commissioner Howard asked whether there had been any communication with members of the new
               federal administration regarding the Essential Air Service (EAS) program. The Airport Director
               responded that there has been no direct contact with the new administration; however, the
               Department of Transportation continues to process EAS and AEAS reimbursements as usual. It was
               noted that Secretary Duffy is familiar with the importance of EAS, having previously represented a
               district in rural Wisconsin with EAS-served airports.

              Commissioner Malmberg inquired about the future of the current Airport Rescue and Fire Fighting
               (ARFF) vehicle once the new unit is placed into service. Staff explained that maintaining both
               vehicles would impose a significant financial burden on the Authority, as both would need to be kept
               operational with reserve firefighting agents—an investment that is not required for an airport of this
               size.

              Commissioner Howard shared observations from his recent visit to Washington, D.C., noting a large
               number of new faces in federal roles and encouraged collaboration with airport staff to build
               relationships with newly appointed officials. Commissioner Hollinger shared his experience with his
               recent visit to Washington, D.C., and shared similar sentiments.

   Chair Finigan requested that the Airport Director give a presentation to the governing bodies of the JPA member
    agencies, providing an update on Advanced Air, their service, plans for the future and new partnership with Alaska
    Airlines.

Re: Exercise Notice for Extension of Airport Master Lease

Dear Mr. Lopez,

Pursuant to the Master Lease Agreement (MLA) between the County of Del Norte and the Border Coast Regional Airport
Authority (County Agreement No. 2011-092), I am writing to formally notify you of the Authority’s intent to exercise its
option to extend the lease of Jack McNamara Field, as provided for under the terms of the MLA.

Specifically, Article 1.3 of the MLA allows for lease extensions in five-year increments following the expiration of the
original term, as well as for any subsequent extensions. The original term of the MLA is set to expire on December 31,
2025. In accordance with the agreement, the Authority must transmit written notice of its intent to exercise an extension at
least 180 days prior to the expiration of the current term.
With this letter, the Authority hereby provides timely notice of its intent to exercise the first extension option, covering the
period from January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2030. This notice is submitted without condition or qualification.

Additionally, in accordance with Section 2.2 of the MLA, following this Exercise Notice, the Authority and the County are
required to meet in good faith to determine a fair and reasonable rent for the airport. This determination should consider the
financial conditions of both the County and the Airport Authority, as outlined in the agreement.

We value our continued partnership with the County and appreciate the opportunity to manage Jack McNamara Field on its
behalf.

Regards,




Ryan Cooley
Airport Director

LEASE AGREEMENT BETWEEN BORDER COAST REGIONAL AIRPORT AUTHORITY AND CAL-
 ORE LIFE FLIGHT FOR FUEL ISLAND AND FUEL FARM AT DEL NORTE COUNTY REGIONAL
                                    AIRPORT

This Lease ("Lease") is made and entered into by and between the Border Coast Regional
Airport Authority, a California Joint Powers AUTHORITY ("the AUTHORITY") and Cal-Ore
Life Flight ("LESSEE") on June 1, 2025.

WHEREAS, the AUTHORITY manages and operates the Del Norte County Regional
AIRPORT, Jack McNamara Field, at Crescent City California, hereinafter referred to as "the
AIRPORT"; and

WHEREAS, The LESSEE desires to lease, operate, and maintain the fuel island and fuel
farm located at the AIRPORT; and

WHEREAS, LESSEE is the Fixed Based Operator at Jack McNamara Field and
understands the rules and regulations that apply to the AIRPORT;

THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein, the parties agree as follows:

1. Premises. The AUTHORITY hereby leases to the LESSEE, and the LESSEE accepts from
   the AUTHORITY, under the terms and conditions outlined in this Lease, the equipment and
   premises located at the airport, described as follows:
       • Two (2) 10,000-gallon above-ground fuel tanks with exposed aggregate finish;
       • Two (2) external turbine pumps and associated fill and supply piping;
       • One (1) fuel dispenser with aviation hose and nozzle;
       • Grounding cable and reel;
       • Fuel island slab and shelter, including filters, floats, and pumps;
       • All underground piping from tanks to the fuel island dispenser.

2. Term. This Lease begins June 1, 2025, and terminates December 31, 2030, unless
   terminated earlier as provided herein. It may be renewed by mutual agreement for up to
   five (5) additional five-year terms, with base rent subject to renegotiation as described in
   this section

   (a) Holding Over. If LESSEE remains in possession after expiration of the Lease with the
       AUTHORITY’s consent and without a written extension, tenancy becomes month-to-
       month under the same terms. Monthly rent during any renewal or holdover period will be
       the amount set by the AUTHORITY at the start of that period.

   (b) Rent. Monthly rent during the initial 12-month term of this Lease is $756.03 beginning
       on June 1, 2025. LESSEE shall pay rent as specified, due on the first day of each
       month and delinquent after the 10th. Partial months will not be prorated. Rent shall be
       paid to the AUTHORITY at 1650 Dale Rupert Rd. Suite 100, Crescent City, CA
       95531.

            •   $0.12 per gallon of Jet A fuel pumped (non-commercial);
            •   $0.12 per gallon of Jet A fuel pumped for commercial air carriers paying landing
                fees at the airport.

         Fuel Flowage Fees may be increased by the Border Coast Regional Airport Authority
Board of Commissioners provided that equal flowage fees are charged to all purveyors of fuel at
the airport and provided that the additional revenue obtained from the increase in flowage fees
is placed in a fund used only for the operation and maintenance of the airport and all associated

for all purveyors of fuel at the airport.

   (c) Rent Adjustments. In accordance with changes to the Consumer Price Index (CPI) by

       Lease beginning in 2025. CPI by Major Groups means the average of "all items" shown

       Department of Labor. The AUTHORITY will give 30 days’ advance written notice of the
       increase. In the event that the basic index (CPI) decreases to the point of reflecting a
       negative figure for that year, the rental rate will not change. If the foregoing index is at
       any time no longer available, then a comparable economic indicator will be selected to
       determine the annual rent adjustment.

   (d) Late Charges. Rent payments not received by AUTHORITY by the 10th day of the
       month are subject to a late penalty of 10 percent (10%) of the total monthly payment but

       the Del Norte County Regional AIRPORT Terminal.

       contributing to this FY 25-26 budget, including Del Norte County Board of Supervisors, and to
       submit the adopted budget to the Del Norte County Auditor-Controller.
   B. The position list for wireless device stipends

SUMMARY:
The Border Coast Regional Airport Authority (BCRAA), acting as a Joint Powers Authority (JPA),
administers operations at three public-use airports on behalf of Del Norte County. BCRAA oversees three
related budget units: 432-433 (BCRAA), 433-434 (Special Aviation), and 142-323 (Airport/PFC), which
collectively support the operation and management of the County’s airport system. The proposed FY
2025–2026 budgets for each unit are attached to this report in full for the Board’s review and
consideration (refer to the “25/26 Dept” column for proposed figures). Overall, the proposed budgets
closely align with those adopted in the prior fiscal year, maintaining consistency in typical operating
revenue and expenditures.

DISCUSSION:
BCRAA Budget Overview (Unit 432-433)
The proposed FY 2025–2026 BCRAA budget (432-433) is included in full as an attachment to this Board
Report for the Board’s consideration (see the “25/26 Dept” column for proposed line items). Overall, the
proposed budget is consistent with those adopted in previous years in terms of standard operating
revenues and expenditures.

Operating Revenues
Normal operating revenue—generated from leases with Contour Airlines, TSA, FedEx, Cal-Ore Life Flight,
hangar tenants, and others—remains stable. Revenue from car rental concessions with Avis and Budget
also continues to perform reliably.

County Contribution
The annual contribution from Del Norte County for airport administration is expected to remain unchanged
at $271,608.
Terminal Loan Status
Under DN CO AGMT #2016-149, Section 5, repayment of the Terminal Loan is contingent upon the
receipt of “Pledged Revenue.” This includes (a) contributions from member entities or other specified
sources solely for the purpose of loan repayment, and (b) funds from the reallocation of aviation fuel sales
tax. As no “Pledged Revenue” has been received, a payment on the terminal loan provided by Del Norte
County has not been budgeted this fiscal year.

Caltrans Loan
The full annual payment of $21,637 on the Caltrans loan has been budgeted.

Salaries & Benefits
Personnel costs reflect full-year staffing of all four allocated positions and include:

Salaries: $347,128 (includes 5% step increases and a 2% contingency for potential COLA adjustments)

Overtime: $5,000

Retirement Contributions: $60,646

Employee Benefits: $59,965

CalPERS Unfunded Accrued Liability (UAL): $70,591

County-Provided Services and Other Key Costs
The following County-related service expenses have been reviewed and entered by the County:

Cost Plan Allocation: $74,989 (up $6,675 from prior year’s $68,314)

Computer Leases: $7,104 (down $2,709 from $9,813)

Property Insurance: $55,244 (down $6,796 from $62,040)

Liability Insurance: $10,313 (up $113 from $10,200)

Workers’ Compensation Insurance: $3,336 (down $163 from $3,499)

Additional standard operating expenses are included as applicable to support ongoing operations.

Nonstandard Expenditures – FY 2025–2026 Budget

The following line items reflect notable nonstandard increases anticipated in the FY 2025–2026 budget:

Professional Services – Legal: $150,000 allocated for ongoing litigation-related costs.

Professional Services – $31,000 to support continued legal and engineering consultant services.

Professional Services - $50,000 allocated for profession services needs including the upcoming
business feasibility and SWOT studies for Jack McNamara Field.
Maintenance – Structural Improvements/Grounds: $45,500 for critical roof repairs on the lower section of
the old terminal building.

Equipment - $9,000 for the purchase and installation of a new transformer and electrical outlet on the
airline apron.

ARFF Services & Supplies: $25,000 for the procurement of a backup supply of Halotron and Argon gas
to support aircraft rescue and firefighting operations.

As the Board is aware, special sources of revenue and expense—such as state and federal grants—can
fluctuate from year to year. Accordingly, mid-year budget amendments (e.g., Budget Transfers) are often
required when grant funding is secured for projects identified in the Airport Capital Improvement Plan
(ACIP).



Currently, two active Airport Improvement Program (AIP) projects are programmed into the budget:

AIP 43 – ARFF Equipment and Vehicle

AIP 48 – Runway 18-36 Rehabilitation (Construction Phase)

For FY 2025–2026, we anticipate incorporating the Beach Front Fence Replacement project, funded
under the Airport Infrastructure Grant (AIG) program, into the budget as well.

In the Special Aviation (433-434) budget, like last year, we are once again including an expenditure of
$1,500 in order to pay for assistance from the Del Norte County Roads Division for work necessary at
Andy McBeth Airport in Klamath and Ward Field in Gasquet such as runway surface cleaning and
transportation of our mowing tractor.

The FY 25-26 budget is presented as a balanced budget.

Under the terms of the JPA agreement BCRAA’s Board of Commissioners is responsible for adopting an
annual operating budget for each of the budget units under its management. Additionally, pursuant to the
JPA agreement, the budget adopted by BCRAA must also be ratified by any member of the JPA that
contributes financially to the JPA. As Del Norte County is presently the only member of the JPA
contributing funds on an annual basis to BCRAA, the Del Norte County Board of Supervisors must also
ratify the budget before it can be considered final. As such, I have contacted the Clerk of the Board of
Supervisors and have requested that BCRAA’s budget be placed on the Board of Supervisors’ agenda for
the regularly scheduled meeting of June 24 in order to be approved before the start of the new fiscal year.
At this time, the staff recommends that the budgets be approved by the BCRAA Board and then
forwarded to the Del Norte County Board of Supervisors for final approval in order to have an adopted
budget in place for the 25-26 fiscal year.

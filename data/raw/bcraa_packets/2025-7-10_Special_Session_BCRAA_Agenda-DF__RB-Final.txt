     AIRPORT DIRECTOR SEARCH AND SELECTION SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Consultancy Agreement (the “Agreement”) is made and entered into by and between Volaire
Aviation, Inc. (the “Consultant”) and Border Coast Regional Airport Authority/Del Norte County
Jack McNamara Field (CEC) (the “Company” and/or “Airport”) (hereinafter referred to
individually as a “Party” and collectively as “the Parties”).

1.     Engagement and Services

(a) Engagement. The Company hereby engages the Consultant to provide and perform the
services set forth in this section of the agreement (the “Services”), and the Consultant hereby
accepts the engagement.

(b) Term. This agreement will begin upon its signing and be in effect until a new Airport
Director has been hired by Company.

(c) Included Scope of Work. Consultant will provide the following Airport Director search and
selection services to the Company:

     1) Job requirements

        Consultant will work with Company to generate a prioritized list of job requirements
        including special qualifications, characteristics, and experience. Consultant will also
        work with Company to develop a detailed job description. The descrption will include
        essential functions to be performed in the role and the advantages of working for the
        company (i.e., workplace environment, compensation and benefits, perks, etc.).

     2) Salary range

        Consultant will develop research from other airports and Airport Director searches to
        recommend a salary range for the position. This research will be indexed by cost of
        living and airport size.

     3) Timeline and hiring plan

        Consultant will develop a hiring plan that includes a timeline for the process and start-
        date of the new director, a recruitment plan and recommendation, criteria for initial
        candidate screening, a plan for those who should be a part of the selection committee, and
        a list of potential interview questions.

     4) Candidate recruitment

        Consultant will work through its airport leadership network to develop interest in the
        position and develop a list of qualified and interested candidates.

     5) Resume review and recommendation

        Consultant will review resumes submitted for the position and vet potential candidates.
        Consultant will narrow the group of resumes to a set of qualified candidates. Consultant
        can also further narrow the list from qualified candidates to recommended candidates.




                                                 1
     6) Interview participation

         Consultant will participate on the Company’s interview panel, and represent air service
         development interests. Consultant will work with panel to ensure the most qualified
         applicant is offered the position.


2.     Consultancy Fee and Expenses

(a) Fee Schedule. Consultant will invoice Company on a per-project basis. Each project will
be invoiced upon completion at the following rates:

     1) Job requirements, salary range, timeline/hiring plan, job posting:                    $7,500
     2) Candidate recruitment:                                                                $5,000
     3) Resume review and recommendation:                                                     $5,000
     4) Candidate interviews and selection (including trip to Airport):                       $4,500

(d) Expenses. Consultant shall be entitled to reimbursement for expenses reasonably incurred
in the performance of the Services, upon submission and approval of written statements in
accordance with the then regular procedures of the Company. Reasonable expenses include, but
are not limited to, travel (airfare, hotel, rental car, and meals), printing of materials, and shipping
of materials. Consultant will invoice all expenses at cost plus a 10% administrative fee.

(e) Payment. The Consultant shall submit to the Company invoices detailing the Services
performed, expenses, and the amount due. All such invoices shall be due and payable within
thirty (30) calendar days after receipt thereof by the Company.

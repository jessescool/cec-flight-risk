Re: Terminal Loan Payment Notification (FY 26-27)
    DN CO AGMT 2016-149

Mr. Lopez,

As stated in Section 4 of the Note and Financing Agreement executed between the County and the Airport Authority the
Authority is obligated to notify the County annually if, in the Authority's opinion, it appears unlikely that "Pledged
Revenue" will be available to make the annually required loan payment. This same section of the Agreement also requires
that the Authority provide notice to the County if a partial payment could possibly be made.

Please consider this letter to be the annual notification from the Authority, satisfying the notification requirement of the
loan agreement, that the Authority will not be able to include funds in its FY 26-27 budget for a full or partial payment to
the County on this loan due to the lack of "Pledged Revenue" for loan repayment.

Please let me know if any additional information is needed from the Authority at this time.




Airport Director

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

June 27, 2024

Ryan Cooley, Airport Director, Border Coast Regional Airport Authority

RE: Border Coast Regional Airport Authority – Alternate Essential Air Service Grant Award

Crescent City, CA – June 27, 2024 – The Border Coast Regional Airport Authority has secured a new
4-year AEAS grant agreement with the DOT. The agreement ensures continued, expanded
connectivity for Crescent City, CA and the surrounding Border Coast Region. Advanced Air, LLC
offers daily round-trip service from Crescent City (CEC) to Oakland (OAK) alongside new twice-
weekly round-trip flights between Crescent City (CEC) and Hawthorne/Los Angeles (HHR).

Since March 2024, multiple collaborative meetings involving The Border Coast Regional Airport
Authority, Mr. Michael Mooney of Volaire Aviation Consulting, Advanced Air, LLC, and the
Department of Transportation have taken place. The primary objective has been to enhance regional
air service ensuring safety, reliability and expanding destination options to boost ridership, stimulate
business growth and promote tourism.

Crucial to these efforts is increased funding from the DOT for Alternate Essential Air Service,
supporting economic development and opportunity in our region. The DOT has awarded a new four-
year AEAS grant totaling $19,312,532, a $4.6 million increase from the previous agreement. This
funding boost will continue to facilitate existing flights to both Oakland (OAK) and Hawthorne/Los
Angeles (HHR), with increased frequency starting Summer of 2025.

This initiative is part of the Department of Transportation's (DOT) Alternate Essential Air Service
(AEAS) Program, originally granted in 2018. The existing agreement is valid through September 30,
2024. The AEAS program ensures continued air service for small communities across the United
States originally served by certificated air carriers pre-1978 deregulation, through subsidies for air
service.

The Border Coast Regional Airport Authority is excited to continue to provide enhanced air service
that will benefit our community and foster regional growth. These developments underscore our
commitment to providing reliable transportation options and advancing economic opportunities for the
Border Coast Region.

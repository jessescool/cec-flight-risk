 SUBJECT:       Capital Improvement Plan 2025-2029

 RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
 Approve airport staff to submit the FY 2025-2029 ACIP to the FAA.

 SUMMARY/DISCUSSION:
 Airport staff prepared an updated ACIP for the FY 2025-2029 period which was presented to the Board at the
 December 7, 2023 meeting. The ACIP is a planning document that is used to identify and prioritize the
 airport's capital needs and is transmitted to the FAA for inclusion in the nationwide ACIP in order for future
 projects to be eligible for AIP and BIL/AIG funding. Once the ACIP is accepted by the FAA, projects identified
 may be funded through the Airport Improvement Program (AIP) or Airport Infrastructure Grant (BIL/AIG).

 Staff is requesting Authority approval to submit the FY 2025-2029 ACIP to the FAA.

RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
Review and approve the Advanced Air Landing Permit and updated insurance terms effective from
March 17, 2024 to September 30, 2024, authorize Chair to sign.**

SUMMARY/DISCUSSION:
This landing permit is a continuation of the terms as negotiated with Contour Airlines.

Section 10 - Insurance has been modified to include updated industry standard terms and amounts.

The original landing permit required:
   • One hundred million dollars ($100,000,000) combined single limit for bodily injury, property
      damage, and personal injury to passengers;
   • Twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) personal injury to third parties other than
      passengers;
   • Five million dollars ($5,000,000) legal liability for fire;
   • Ten million dollars ($10,000,000), which may be in the form of excess liability insurance, for
      automobile, employer's liability, advertisers liability; Ten million dollars ($10,000,000) aviation
      general liability coverage.

The Advanced Air landing permit requires
   • One hundred million dollars ($100,000,000) combined single limit for bodily injury, property
      damage, including bodily injury to passengers;
   • Twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000) for liability for personal injury, including advertising
      injury;
   • Twenty million dollars ($20,000,000) premises liability for third-party bodily injury and property
      damage and;
   • Twenty million dollars ($20,000,000) for on-airport auto liability

                                            Recitals

      WHEREAS, under a Master Lease, the Border Coast Regional Airport Authority
(“Authority”) possesses and manages the Del Norte County Regional Airport (Jack
McNamara Field), ("AIRPORT”) located near Crescent City, California; and

         WHEREAS, the PERMITTEE desires to operate a commercial flight service that will
utilize the AIRPORT; and

WHEREAS, the Authority and the PERMITTEE are parties to that certain Agreement for
Air Services dated January 3, 2024 that, in part, further describes the commercial flight service
to be operated by PERMITEE at the AIRPORT, (the “ASA”) and that certain Terminal
Building Office Space Lease Agreement dated ___________, 2024 (the “Lease”); and

WHEREAS, the Authority is a party to that certain Grant Agreement with the United States
Department of Transportation dated October 1, 2020 which provides a portion of the funding
required for PERMITTEE to operate the commercial air service at the AIRPORT as further
described in the ASA. Together, the Grant, the ASA and the Lease are the “Related
Agreements”

        NOW, THEREFORE, for good and valuable consideration, the receipt and
sufficiency of which is hereby acknowledged, the Authority and the PERMITTEE mutually
agree:

       1. TERM: The Term of this Permit will commence on the Effective Date and will
continue until the earlier of (i) the termination or expiration of any of the Related
Agreements; or (ii) September 30, 2024

        2. SERVICES: Under this Permit, PERMITTEE is allowed to carry on commercial
flight activities at the AIRPORT.
    3.      FEES: Subject to (i) the negotiation, execution, and ratification of the Related
Agreements; (ii) the satisfaction of all conditions precedent under this Permit and the
Related Agreements; and (iii) the actual commencement of revenue flights under the ASA,
from and after the date of the PERMITTEE's first revenue flight under the ASA through the
end of the Term of this Permit, PERMITTEE will pay to AUTHORITY a landing fee based
upon the extent of use of the AIRPORT by PERMITTEE's aircraft. This fee will be
computed for each calendar month or portion thereof at the rate of $1.90 per thousand
(1,000) pounds gross landing weight landed by PERMITTEE at the Airport, computed in
accordance with this Permit. Additionally, subject to (i) the negotiation, execution, and
ratification of the Related Agreements; (ii) the satisfaction of all conditions precedent under
this Permit and the Related Agreements; and (iii) the actual commencement of revenue
flights under the ASA, from and after the date of the PERMITTEE's first revenue flight
under the ASA through the end of the Term of this Permit, PERMITTEE shall pay to
AUTHORITY a fee of $5,000.00 per calendar month to cover expenses to support Airport
Rescue and Firefighting requirements, which fee for partial calendar months will be
prorated.

          The term "gross landing weight" for any aircraft, as used herein, means the
 maximum landing weight approved by the Federal Aviation Agency for landing that
 aircraft at the AIRPORT. All of PERMITTEE's revenue-producing and training flights
 into the AIRPORT, both scheduled and unscheduled, are included in the computation of
 gross landing weight for each calendar month. All training flights are considered as one
 flight, regardless of the number of landings and take-offs during the flight.

         To determine and provide for the payment of the landing fees, during the Term of
 this Permit, PERMITTEE will keep accurate records in a form reasonably satisfactory to
 AUTHORITY of all landings at the AIRPORT by revenue producing and training flights
 of PERMITTEE. Not later than the tenth calendar day of each month, PERMITTEE
 will render to AUTHORITY a statement from such records, showing the total number of
 such landings during the preceding calendar month, together with the gross landing
 weight of each. The statement must further show the amount payable to AUTHORITY
 a n d c o n c u r r e n t l y remit the amount shown to be due.

        Landing fees become delinquent ten (10) days after the due date and will be
 assessed a 5% late fee. In addition, any accounts remaining unpaid sixty (60) days after
 the due date will thereafter bear interest at the rate of ten percent (10%) per annum.

        The landing fee is subject to increase in accordance with the changes in the
 Consumer Price Index by Major Groups. The Consumer Price Index by Major Groups
 means the average of "all items" shown on the United States City Average for Urban
 Wage Earners and Clerical Workers as promulgated by the Bureau of Labor Statistics of
 the United States Department of Labor. Any increases will take effect on October 1 of
 each calendar year and only after thirty (30) days' written notice to the PERMITTEE of
 the amount of the increase.

Airports are required to provide quarterly updates regarding their open AIP projects to their ADO
Program Manager to give an accurate picture as to where each airport stands regarding
utilization of their funding, project status and to ensure airports are not simply sitting on their
allotted funds.

This year we have several open grants associated with projects that are ongoing which are
detailed more fully below. We have many projects and project phases either underway and/or
nearing completion and we will continue to move forward to ensure our AIP projects are
completed on time or ahead of schedule.

Board for review. Details specific to the various AIP projects are provided below.

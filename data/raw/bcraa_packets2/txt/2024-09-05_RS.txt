                                              Scope of Work
INTRODUCTION

This Scope of work encompasses the bidding and construction administration services that will be provided by
WHPacific Inc./NV5 (Engineer), to the Border Coast Regional Airport Authority (Owner) for the project work
entitled Runway 18-36 Rehabilitation, Marking, and Lighting Upgrade Improvements at Del Norte County
Regional – Jack McNamara Field Airport (CEC). Design services were provided under a separate contract and
grant funding.

The Runway 18-36, Marking, and Lighting Upgrade project is the rehabilitation of deteriorated runway
pavement, a new medium intensity lighting system (MIRL), guidance signs, and marking improvements to the
runway. Drainage improvements are not included in the design scope of work. Electrical improvements were
made to the electrical building along with the routing of power for the systems as part of these improvements.

Engineering services will be performed following guidance under the current FAA requirements for use of AIP
funds and under the Master Services Agreement between WHPacific Inc./NV5 and the Border Coast Regional
Airport Authority. The following is a summary of the services to be provided by Engineer to the BCRAA for the
above Project.

                                                  BOARD MEMO

DATE: August 30, 2024                           AGENDA DATE:          September 5, 2024
MEMO TO:        Border Coast Regional Airport Authority Board of Commissioners
FROM:           Ryan Cooley, Airport Director
SUBJECT:        Runway 18-36 Project Update

RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
Receive update on the upcoming Runway 18-36 Rehabilitation, Lighting and Marking Project.

DISCUSSION/SUMMARY:
On Monday, August 26, a meeting was held with airport staff, Tidewater Contractors, and our project engineer, Mr.
John Shute, PE, from WHPacific. After careful consideration, it was determined that due to the late awarding of grant
funds, it would be most beneficial to postpone the construction phase of the project to late spring or early summer
2025.

As outlined in the attached draft timeline, if we were to proceed with construction this year, paving activities would
extend into late December 2024. Paving during the rainy season in our coastal climate presents significant risks, as it
cannot be performed under wet conditions. Attempting to pave during this period would likely result in suboptimal
outcomes, extended construction time, and other complications associated with cold and wet weather. Furthermore,
pavement markings require consistent temperatures above 55°F for proper application.

Postponing the construction will not only lead to better overall results but will also allow sufficient time for the
procurement and delivery of the MIRL LED lighting system, which has a potentially lengthy lead time. This will ensure
the lighting system can be installed without delays.

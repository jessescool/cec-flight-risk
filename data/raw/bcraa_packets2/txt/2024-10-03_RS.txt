SUBJECT:        Wind Direction Indicator Payment

RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
Approve a fixed asset payment in the amount of $9,438.09 for the purchase and installation of a new secondary wind direction
Indicator, as budgeted for in the FY 2024-2025 BCRAA approved budget, at the request of the Del Norte County Auditor-
Controller’s office. **

DISCUSSION/SUMMARY:
In March of 2024 the secondary wind direction indicator at Jack McNamara Field was damaged beyond repair by a large
winter storm. This is a mandatory airfield nav-aid required by the FAA. The procurement and installation were budgeted
for and included in the approved FY 24-25 budget. The Del Norte County Auditor-Controller’s office is requiring board
approval of the payment as it is considered a Fixed Asset line item.

Do not approve the payment.

WHAT ISA MICROGRID
                                       --------
A Microgrid is an interconnected, self-sufficient energy system within a clearly defined electrical
boundary that can act as a single controllable entity. It can connect and disconnect from the grid to
operate in grid-connected or island mode.


                                                               Microgrids provide energy resilience to grid
                                                               disturbances, improve local government +
                                                               critical community service reliability

                                                               By disconnecting from the larger electric grid
                                                               during outages + providing power to
                                                               customers within the boundary of the microgrid
                                                               utilizing local energy-generating resources
                                                               such as solar panels, batteries, generators

SUBJECT: Airport Capital Improvement Plan 2025-2030

RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
Receive update on the proposed FY 2025-2030 ACIP from the FAA.

SUMMARY/DISCUSSION:
Airport staff in partnership with the FAA Airports District Office (ADO) has updated the draft ACIP for 2025-
2030 as of November 25, 2024. The ACIP is a planning document that is used to identify and prioritize the
airport's capital needs and is transmitted to the FAA for inclusion in the nationwide ACIP in order for future
projects to be eligible for AIP and BIL/AIG funding. Once the ACIP is accepted by the FAA, projects identified
may be funded through the Airport Improvement Program (AIP) or Airport Infrastructure Grant (BIL/AIG).


1. 2025 Projects
      • Beach Front Fence Replacement
         FAA Approved – Pre Application Checklist Submitted
      • ALP Master Plan
         FAA Unapproved - Not required at the moment; need to save discretionary and entitlement
         funds for future runway/taxiway rehab. May make minor changes to ALP if needed through
         the ALP Pen & Ink process. Push out to 2029
2. 2026 Projects
      • RWY 12-30 Runway Rehab and Lighting Upgrade Project (Phase 1 –Design)
         FAA Approved: Initially the FAA requested that the lighting upgrades be done at a later date
         but after clarification from airport staff that due to the operational impact of the project,
         multiple closures will cause delays and long periods of time without aircraft operations, that
         including the lighting upgrades at the same time as the pavement rehabilitation will be vital
         to minimizing the operational impact on all air service at the airport.
3. 2027 Projects
      • RWY 12-30 Runway Rehab and Lighting Upgrade Project (Phase 2 – Construction)
         FAA Approved: See Phase 1 Design Notes from 2026 projects.
       •   TWY A&B Redesign and Rehabilitation (Phase 1 – Design)
           FAA Requested to push out to 2028 due to limited FAA discretionary
           funding.
       •   Relocate and Replace Primary Wind Direction Indicator
           FAA Requested to push out to 2028 due to limited FAA discretionary
           funding.
       •   Replace Rotating Beacon
           FAA requested to push out to 2028 due to
           limited discretionary funding.

 SUBJECT: Update on Adjustment to Entitlement Amounts for Nonprimary Commercial Service
          Airports


 RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
 Receive update on entitlement fund calculations for nonprimary commercial service airports.

 SUMMARY/DISCUSSION:
 Recent legislative changes have updated the entitlement calculations for nonprimary commercial
 service airports, defined as those with annual enplanements between 2,500 and 9,999. These
 airports are now eligible to receive entitlement amounts ranging from $150,000 to $1.3 million, based
 on the number of enplanements. The revised calculation allocates funding as follows:

 $60 per enplanement for the first 2,500 passenger boardings
 $153.33 per enplanement for the next 7,499 passenger boardings

 Example Calculation:
 Based on CEC enplanement statistics as of November 2024 (7,301 enplanements)

 $60 × 2,500 = $150,000
 $153.33 × 4,801 = $736,137
 Total Entitlement: $886,137

 Important Note:
 While this legislation has been signed into law, funding for these adjustments has not yet been
 appropriated. Further updates will be provided as additional information becomes available.

 SUBJECT: Del Norte County - Appointed Department Head Salary Adjustment Update


 RECOMMENDATION FOR BOARD ACTION:
 Receive update on Del Norte County - Appointed Department Head Resolution 2024-036

 SUMMARY/DISCUSSION:
 On November 12, 2024, the Del Norte County Board of Supervisors approved Resolution No. 2024-
 036, implementing a 5% wage adjustment for appointed department heads. This adjustment took
 effect starting with the pay period beginning November 8, 2024. All other provisions of Resolution
 No. 2023-016 remain in place until the Board adopts a full successor resolution.

 This adjustment applies to the Airport Director due to BCRAA Resolution No. 2019-02, which
 mandates that any current or future agreements governing the benefits of authority employees,
 including the Airport Director, established between the Del Norte County Department Head
 Bargaining Unit and the County of Del Norte, are to be adopted by BCRAA.
